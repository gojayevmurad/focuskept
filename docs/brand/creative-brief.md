# Creative brief

Status: direction **A / Side by Side** selected by the owner on 23 November 2026. The [vector kit](../../brand/README.md) implements that direction and is prepared for review. The alternatives below document the comparison that preceded the selection.

## Brand foundation

**Name:** FocusKept. Preserve capitalization and the existing domain/repository identity.

**Category descriptor:** Virtual coworking and body doubling.

**Promise:** Make time for your work, with someone working beside you.

**Personality:** Calm, companionable, precise, dependable. Warmth should come from language and human context; discipline should come from spacing, timing, and consistency.

**Proposed primary line:** “Quiet company. Focus kept.” This is draft copy, not a claim of trademark clearance.

**Existing expressive headline:** “Your lane. Someone in the next one.” Retain as a useful candidate, supported immediately by a plain explanation of the video session. A metaphor must not make a newcomer think this is a swimming or sports product.

**Plain supporting copy:** “Book 25, 50, or 75 minutes. Meet a partner on video, share your goal, and work on your own tasks together.”

**Developer supporting line:** “Open-source virtual coworking. Use FocusKept or host your own.”

Use source availability, privacy controls, and clear session mechanics as proof. Do not frame unlimited productivity, medical treatment, guaranteed partners, or unmeasured performance improvements as product promises.

Avoid overlapping stock circles, generic checkmarks, targets, brains, infinity signs, handshakes, AI sparkles, and ornamental gradients as the identity's main idea. A camera is a feature symbol, not automatically a useful brand symbol. Any deliberate use of a common motif must have a visible product-specific transformation.

## Recommended visual territory

Develop A first while showing B and C alongside it. Preserve teal and deep water as candidates because they already organize the app, not because a color is proven to “build trust.”

| Role                                | Starting candidate    | Treatment                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Primary action / identifying accent | Pool teal `#08646b`   | Dominant brand hue; derive usable states with measured contrast               |
| Main ink                            | Deep water `#102f43`  | Clear text, one-color mark, wordmark                                          |
| Light ground                        | Mist `#f4f8fa`        | Calm background; keep large areas uncluttered                                 |
| Dark ground                         | Night water `#081c2a` | Intentional dark application, not a blind inversion                           |
| Dark-theme accent                   | Light teal `#7bcdd2`  | Existing readable dark accent                                                 |
| Timing highlight                    | Rope red `#d23b4e`    | Small, controlled timing detail; not required to recognize the mark           |
| Secondary equipment detail          | Yellow `#f3cc45`      | Sparse decoration with an adequate alternative when communicating information |

The final identity should work in one ink. Red and yellow are optional expression devices, not tiny mandatory details in every favicon. At small sizes, eliminate detail rather than rendering the full complex mark badly. Keep red/yellow distinct from the application's separate error/warning semantics.

Retain Figtree for reading and Big Shoulders for display in the first concept pass. Develop wordmark spacing and details independently; do not simply typeset the name and declare a final logo. Test Big Shoulders headings for excessive athletic/aggressive tone. An alternate family is justified only by visible improvement in the same specimens and verified license/glyph coverage.

Use `0123456789`, `25 / 50 / 75`, `09:58`, `11:11`, and `50:00` in type tests. Timers need tabular numerals. Test real strings in English, German, French, Dutch, and Spanish. If Azerbaijani/Turkish samples are included, treat them as future glyph coverage checks, not shipped language support.

## Voice

| Context               | Prefer                            | Avoid                                                     |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Primary action        | “Book a session”                  | A metaphor-only CTA that hides the video booking          |
| Session start         | “What will you work on?”          | “Unlock your peak performance”                            |
| Incomplete goal       | “A little progress still counts.” | Guilt, broken-streak pressure, or moral judgments         |
| Partner unavailable   | Explain the actual options        | Promising someone will always be there                    |
| Developer entry point | “Host your own” / “Contribute”    | Treating infrastructure as the benefit for every end user |

Keep the UI's action vocabulary stable. A stylistic change does not justify rewriting every translation or existing SEO title. Label new example people and stats as sample data.

## Concept-board requirements

Each direction must show its mechanism in one sentence, a mark on light/dark grounds, outlined horizontal wordmark lockup, a 16/24/32 px strip at actual display size, app tile with mask crop, current header and calendar context, email header, and one 1200×630 card. Include a monochrome version. If a board is image-generated, it is exploratory art; export geometry, spellings, tiny sizes, and contrast still need independent production work.

Assess recall and distinctiveness, readability, relationship to the session, emotional tone, one-color performance, small-size survival, and fit with the implemented Lanes system. Explicitly test the “pause icon” and “sports app” misreadings for A. Do not call any route unique or trademark-safe based only on a web comparison.

## Working stages

1. Present A/B/C with a recommendation and concrete tradeoffs.
2. Record the owner's selection, refine that route in context, and record any explicitly approved mix.
3. Produce the complete kit in [asset-spec.md](asset-spec.md), including clearspace, minimum sizes, background rules, misuses, source geometry, fonts/notices, and reproducible exports.
4. Integrate with the handoff prompt and inspect the result across the actual product.

The owner requested research before creation. This document completes that planning stage; it does not authorize recording a direction as approved or publishing an identity.
