# FocusKept Content Strategy & Editorial Plan

This document outlines the organic content marketing strategy, topic cluster architecture, published post index, 12-week editorial calendar, and publication standards for FocusKept.

---

## 1. Goal and Target Audience

### Primary Goals

- **Organic & AI Search Acquisition**: Drive high-intent traffic from Google, Bing, and AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini) to FocusKept's core product, topic hubs, and programmatic landing pages.
- **Conversion to Active Sessions**: Convert searchers experiencing isolation, task paralysis, or exam stress into registered users taking advantage of FocusKept's free plan (3 sessions/week, 25/50/75-minute 1:1 video sessions matched 5 minutes prior).
- **Open-Source Brand Authority**: Establish FocusKept as the transparent, privacy-first, community-driven alternative to venture-backed, paywalled coworking tools across 5 languages (`en`, `de`, `fr`, `nl`, `es`).

### Target Audiences

1. **Remote Workers**: Distributed employees and digital nomads battling work-from-home loneliness, kitchen-table distractions, and unstructured afternoons.
2. **Students & Test Takers**: University students and high-stakes examinees (Bar exam, MCAT, USMLE, CPA, LSAT) requiring silent, disciplined study partners.
3. **People with ADHD & Neurodivergent Adults**: Individuals experiencing executive dysfunction, initiation inertia, and dopamine dysregulation who benefit from body doubling and non-judgmental parallel play.
4. **Writers & Creatives**: Authors, bloggers, and copywriters fighting blank-page dread and needing uninterrupted deep work sprints.
5. **Founders & Solo Operators**: Early-stage entrepreneurs juggling fragmented responsibilities who need dedicated, distraction-free time blocks to complete needle-moving tasks.
6. **Freelancers & Contractors**: Independent consultants managing their own time and client deliverables without an office accountability structure.
7. **Researchers & PhD Candidates**: Academics writing literature reviews, analyzing datasets, or finishing dissertations that require multi-hour cognitive endurance.

## 5. Refresh Rules & Content Audits

Maintaining freshness and factual accuracy ensures continuous indexing, higher CTR, and authoritative citations in AI answer engines:

1. **6-Month Editorial Cadence**:
   - Every published blog post must be reviewed every 6 months.
   - Verify all temporal markers, active years, and UI references (session lengths 25/50/75 minutes, matching window 5 minutes prior, free plan of 3 sessions/week).
   - Check internal links for redirects, broken anchors, or newer relevant programmatic pages.
   - Update frontmatter `date` or add `updatedDate` when making substantial revisions.
2. **Competitor Fact & Pricing Audits**:
   - Re-verify third-party features, subscription costs, and session models.
   - Ensure competitor details are explicitly timestamped (e.g., _"At the time of writing in November 2026..."_).
3. **Google Search Console CTR Optimization**:
   - Filter Performance reports for queries with high impressions (>500/month) but low CTR (<2.5%).
   - Front-load high-intent keywords in the `<title>` (under 60 characters) and meta description (under 155 characters).
   - Test question-based or outcome-driven headlines that directly solve search intent.

## 6. Distribution Checklist per Article

Follow this 4-step checklist upon merging any new post in `content/blog/<slug>.md`:

- [ ] **1. Bi-Directional Internal Linking**:
  - Add contextual links to the new article from at least **two existing published blog posts**.
  - Ensure the new article links back to its parent **topic cluster hub page** (e.g., `/body-doubling-for/adhd` or `/alternatives`) and at least **one programmatic page** (e.g., `/study-with-me/mcat`).
- [ ] **2. Social Channel Distribution with UTM Tracking**:
  - Share the post across FocusKept's active social channels (Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Mastodon, Reddit communities) using standardized tracking URLs:
    ```
    https://focuskept.com/blog/<slug>?utm_source=<platform>&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=blog
    ```
    _(Replace `<platform>` with `twitter`, `linkedin`, `mastodon`, or `reddit`)._
- [ ] **3. Instant IndexNow Submission**:
  - Run the automated IndexNow script to notify Bing, Yandex, and Seznam immediately:
    ```bash
    node scripts/indexnow.mjs https://focuskept.com/blog/<slug>
    ```
- [ ] **4. AI Context Synchronization (`llms.txt`)**:
  - Verify that the new post title, summary, and URL are automatically reflected in:
    - `https://focuskept.com/llms.txt`
    - `https://focuskept.com/llms-full.txt`
  - Ensure Next.js build output includes the post in `sitemap.xml` with the correct `lastModified` date.

## 7. Editorial & Quality Rules

Every article published on FocusKept must strictly adhere to these quality standards:

1. **Immediate Answer in First Two Sentences**:
   - Directly answer the title's core premise or search intent in the opening 1–2 sentences.
   - Avoid long preambles, historical retrospectives, or filler introductions.
2. **Executive TL;DR**:
   - Include a concise 2–3 bullet point summary box (`> [!NOTE]`) at the top of the article summarizing key takeaways for rapid scanning.
3. **Structured FAQ Section**:
   - Conclude every article with 3–5 real searcher questions and direct answers formatted for Schema.org `FAQPage` inclusion.
4. **Zero Invented Data or Fictional Testimonials**:
   - Never generate fake user quotes, fictional partner names, or unsubstantiated statistics.
   - Reference established research (e.g., CHADD, American Psychological Association, cognitive psychology literature) or verified product data.
5. **Fair, Timestamped Competitor Coverage**:
   - Present competitor features and limitations accurately and respectfully.
   - Always append the qualifier _"at the time of writing"_ (with month and year) when mentioning competitor pricing tiers or policies.
   - Emphasize FocusKept's objective attributes: open-source codebase, no paywalled video features, 25/50/75-minute lengths, 5-minute pre-session matching, free 3 sessions/week, and multi-language support (`en`, `de`, `fr`, `nl`, `es`).
6. **No AI Clichés or Fluff**:
   - Prohibit hollow filler phrasing: _"In today's fast-paced digital landscape"_, _"delve into"_, _"game-changer"_, _"tapestry"_, _"beacon of productivity"_, or _"it's important to remember"_.
   - Use clean, pragmatic, active voice written by practitioners for practitioners.
