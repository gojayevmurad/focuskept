## The mechanics of virtual body doubling

Many people find that beginning a difficult task is the hardest part of the workday. A blank calendar slot or an ambitious list rarely creates momentum on its own. Working alongside another person provides a gentle boundary that helps you sit down and start.

This arrangement is commonly called body doubling. Research on social facilitation dating back to Norman Triplett in 1898 showed that people often work with greater persistence when others are present. Stating a concrete plan also draws on implementation intentions research by Peter Gollwitzer, which shows that naming a specific starting action helps bridge the gap between intending to act and doing so.

Body doubling is a practical routine rather than a medical treatment. It can help some people, including remote workers, students, writers, and people with ADHD, establish steady working habits. It is not a substitute for professional care when health is involved.

## Choosing your session length and format

Sessions on {app} run for 25, 50, or 75 minutes and start on the hour or half hour. You can choose the duration that matches your energy and the scope of your work.

A 25-minute session works well for answering difficult emails, clearing administrative backlog, or starting a task you have avoided. This duration mirrors the interval popularized by Francesco Cirillo in the Pomodoro Technique. It keeps the commitment short enough to feel manageable.

A 50-minute session provides room for drafting documents or analyzing data, while a 75-minute block accommodates deep research or complex problem-solving. In addition to 1:1 sessions, you can join hosted group sessions when you prefer a larger room. The interface is available in English, German, French, Dutch, and Spanish.

## Preparing your workspace and materials

A few minutes of quiet preparation before the clock starts makes the session more effective. Choose one well-defined task with an obvious beginning. Deciding what you will do in advance prevents you from spending the opening minutes wondering where to begin.

Set up your physical desk so you do not need to leave the room. Bring a glass of water, plug in your computer, and open your reference documents. Keep confidential paperwork outside your camera view.

Check your video and audio settings before the scheduled time. Every member on {app} must be at least 18 years old. Camera presence is required throughout the call, while your microphone is only needed during spoken check-ins.

## Partner matching five minutes before (T-5)

Five minutes before the session begins, our system pairs you with a partner who booked the same duration. The matching happens automatically in the background.

At this five-minute mark, a join button appears on your dashboard. Entering the room early gives you time to check your camera feed and settle in before the hour or half hour strikes. You will see your partner's first name and general location if they chose to share it.

If your partner does not arrive by the start time, you do not need to cancel your session. When someone is delayed, we try to find you another partner right away. If no alternate partner is available, you can join a hosted group session or continue working solo with the timer running.

## The opening check-in (minutes 0 to 3)

When the session begins, both cameras turn on for a brief greeting lasting two to three minutes. You say hello to your partner and state the specific task you plan to complete.

Frame your goal as an observable action rather than a vague intention. Saying "I will write the introduction paragraph for my report" gives your partner a clear understanding of your plan. Stating a concrete target also helps hold your attention accountable to a defined milestone.

Listen attentively while your partner shares their task. There is no need to give advice, critique their strategy, or offer unsolicited feedback. A polite nod and a friendly word are all that are needed before beginning.

## The quiet focus block: working side by side

Once the opening check-in ends, microphones mute automatically. This removes typing sounds, shuffling papers, and household noise, creating an atmosphere of shared quiet.

You keep your camera on during the focus period. Seeing another person quietly engaged in their own work provides a visual anchor that discourages impulse browsing. You do not need to watch your partner continuously; keeping the window open in your peripheral vision is enough.

Distractions and wandering thoughts can still happen during deep work. Keep a notepad nearby to jot down unrelated ideas for later. You can unmute your microphone if you need to ask a brief logistical question, though most sessions remain silent.

## The wrap-up check-in: reviewing progress

When the timer enters the final two to three minutes, a visual prompt signals that the quiet block is complete. This alert gives you a clean moment to save your work, close active tabs, and note your next step.

The app prompts both participants to mark their outcome by selecting done, partly done, or not done. You then unmute your microphone to share a brief sentence summarizing what you achieved. Your partner does the same, giving each of you an opportunity to acknowledge the effort.

Marking a task as partly done simply reflects the reality of how long complex tasks take. Thank your partner for their company and exit the call promptly. Ending on time keeps the boundaries of each session clear and predictable.

## FAQ

### Do I have to speak on microphone throughout the session?

No, microphones are muted automatically during the quiet focus period. You only speak aloud for a minute or two during the opening goal-sharing and the closing check-in. If you prefer not to speak, you can also communicate your goal using the in-session text chat.

### What happens if my partner does not show up?

If your partner is absent when the session begins, we try to find you another partner right away. You can also choose to transfer into a hosted group session if one is currently active. If no other partner is free, you can continue your quiet work block solo while using the on-screen session timer.

### Are video sessions ever recorded by {app}?

No, video sessions on {app} are never recorded. The video stream is transmitted live between participants and is never stored on our servers. You can work with the assurance that your workspace and conversations remain private.

### How do reporting and blocking work during a call?

If someone violates community standards or acts inappropriately, you can report them with a single click. Submitting a report automatically blocks that user in the same step. The blocked user is permanently barred from matching with you in any future 1:1 sessions.

### How many sessions are included on the free plan?

The Free plan includes 3 sessions per week at no cost, which renews automatically every week. If you need more frequent coworking blocks, a paid Plus plan is available. Both plans provide access to standard 25, 50, and 75-minute sessions.
